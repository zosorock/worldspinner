# 🌍 World Spinner - A World Explorer Game

### 🎮 Core Idea
A geography-based adventure where a young explorer unlocks countries, ancient civilizations, and space mysteries while collecting fascinating facts, animals, foods, and cultural expressions.

---

### 🧭 Game Flow
1. **Spin the World Wheel 🎡** – Each spin reveals a mystery country.
2. **Clue Phase 🕵️‍♂️** – The player receives three progressive clues:
   - 🐾 An animal from that country
   - 🍲 A traditional food
   - 🚩 The national flag (last hint)
3. **Guess Phase ✍️** – They type in the country name. Correct guesses earn points and unlock a new section on their explorer map.
4. **Reward Phase 🎉** – A “Discovery Card” appears with:
   - 🐘 A unique animal fact
   - 💬 A local greeting or phrase
   - 🦕 A dinosaur or fossil found in that region
   - 🏺 A short historical note (e.g., “North Macedonia was once part of Yugoslavia” or “The Franks ruled much of Europe in the early Middle Ages.”)
   - 🪐 A space or science connection (e.g., “This country helped build part of the International Space Station.”)

---

### 🌍 Progression System
- **Explorer Passport & Stamps**: Each correct country gives a stamp. Completing all countries on one continent unlocks that continent’s colorful badge.
- **World Puzzle Map 🧩**: Every correct answer fills in a piece of Earth’s map. Finishing a continent lights it up; completing the world unlocks an animated globe.
- **Continental Rewards**:
  - **Africa** → “Savanna Specialist” badge 🦓
  - **Europe** → “History Hero” badge 🏰
  - **Asia** → “Cultural Voyager” badge 🏯
  - **Americas** → “Nature Guardian” badge 🦜
  - **Oceania** → “Ocean Explorer” badge 🐠
  - **Antarctica** → “Polar Scientist” badge 🐧

- **Explorer Levels**: The player advances from *Junior Adventurer* → *World Pathfinder* → *Planet Geographer* → *Cosmic Voyager*.

---

### 🧩 Learning & Exploration Themes
- **Animals & Nature** – Discover species and habitats unique to each region.
- **Cultural Life** – Learn about local traditions, foods, and greetings.
- **History Through Time** – Introduce ancient kingdoms and modern nations:
  - Ancient Greece → birthplace of democracy 🇬🇷
  - The Franks → foundation of modern France 🇫🇷
  - Mesopotamia → cradle of civilization 🇮🇶
  - Yugoslavia → countries that emerged from it 🇲🇰 🇷🇸 🇭🇷
- **Anthropology** – Explore early human sites, fossils, and cultural artifacts.
- **Astronomy & Space** – Connect geography to the cosmos, like countries that launched satellites, built telescopes, or have constellations named after their myths.

---

### ✨ Optional Features
- **Sound & Visuals** – Fun whooshes, celebration sounds, and cheerful colors (with vibrant teal 💙💚).
- **Mini Missions** – “Find all countries with volcanoes” 🌋 or “Unlock 5 countries that speak Spanish.”
- **Offline Play** – Printable world map puzzle where stickers or stamps can be placed as progress.
- **Game Host Capybara** – A friendly capybara character that acts as the game host, providing clues, encouragement, and fun facts in a whimsical way.

---

### 🗣️ Communication Style
- Tailor all in-game text, clues, facts, and messages to be fun, engaging, and intellectually stimulating for a smart child aged 7 to 12.
- Use exciting language that introduces interesting concepts and vocabulary, without oversimplifying (e.g., "Incredible! You've uncovered the habitat of the elusive snow leopard!").
- Keep it friendly and encouraging to spark curiosity and deeper learning.

---

### 📱 Technical Requirements & Platform
- Develop as a web-based game that runs directly in the browser for easy access.
- Ensure it's responsive and works seamlessly on laptops, phones, and tablets.
- For the initial version, focus on single-player mode.
- Plan the architecture for future multiplayer expansion, similar to Jeopardy, with competitive guessing among players.
